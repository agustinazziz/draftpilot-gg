What it does

Reads the live draft

Your team, the enemy team, bans, and your assigned role — including autofill. No manual input.

Recommends your pick

Ranks the best option from your champion pool plus the two strongest picks outside it, adjusted to the enemy composition, your team's damage and engage balance, the lane matchup, and current-patch win and pick rates.

Explains the pick

A short rationale, an early / mid / late game plan tailored to the enemies you're facing, and ultimate-usage advice against the key threats.

Optional AI coach

Sends the current draft to an AI model for a natural-language read of the game, with its own recommended pick and alternatives.

How it works

  1. Runs as an overlay during champ select, on top of the League client.
  2. Uses only information already visible to you in the client, plus public champion statistics.
  3. It does not automate anything, modify the game, or interact with it in any way — it only reads and advises.
  4. Set your champion pool once; the analysis adapts to every draft automatically.

FAQ

Is this allowed?

DraftPilot only reads information that is already on your screen in champ select, plus publicly available statistics. It never automates gameplay, never modifies the game, and never provides information that other players don't have. It is built for the Overwolf platform and is pending approval from Riot Games.

Does it play the game for me?

No. It does not pick, ban, accept queues, or interact with the client. It is a read-only analysis tool.

What data leaves my computer?

The heuristic recommendations run entirely on your machine. If you enable the AI coach, the current draft state (champions, roles, bans — no personal identifiers) is sent to our service to generate the analysis. See our Privacy Policy.

Which regions and queues?

Ranked Solo/Duo and Flex on the live client. More detail at launch.
