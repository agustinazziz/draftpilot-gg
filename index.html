Your champ select copilot

DraftPilot reads the pick/ban phase as it happens and an AI tells you what to pick — from your champion pool and beyond — from what's already on your screen and current-patch statistics.

What it does

Reads the live draft

Your team, the enemy team, bans, and your assigned role — including autofill. No manual input.

AI recommends your pick

An AI model reads the full draft — your pool, both team compositions, bans, damage and engage balance, the lane matchup, current-patch win and pick rates — and names the pick that fits, from your pool plus the strongest options outside it.

Explains it, plans the game

A short rationale, an early / mid / late plan tailored to the exact enemies, ultimate-usage advice against the key threats, and an item build and rune page from current high-elo data.

Adapts as the draft unfolds

Re-analyzes on every pick and ban. When you lock in, it drops the alternatives and shows only your champion's build and game plan.

How it works

  1. Runs as an overlay during champ select, on top of the League client.
  2. Reads only information already visible to you in the client — allied and enemy picks, bans, your role.
  3. Sends that draft state — champion names, roles, bans, no account or personal data — plus current-patch statistics to an AI model, which produces the recommendation and the plan.
  4. It does not automate anything, modify the game, or interact with it in any way — it only reads and advises.
  5. Set your champion pool once; the analysis runs itself on every draft.

FAQ

Is this allowed?

DraftPilot only reads information that is already on your screen in champ select, plus publicly available statistics. It never automates gameplay, never modifies the game, and never provides information that other players don't have — it just analyzes what everyone in the lobby can already see. It is built for the Overwolf platform and is pending approval from Riot Games.

Does it play the game for me?

No. It does not pick, ban, accept queues, or interact with the client. It is a read-only analysis tool.

What data leaves my computer?

To produce a recommendation, the current draft state — champion names, roles and bans, with no account identifiers and no personal data — is sent to our service and forwarded to a third-party AI provider. The response comes back to your client and is not stored. Nothing else leaves your machine. See our Privacy Policy.

Which regions and queues?

Ranked Solo/Duo and Flex on the live client. More detail at launch.
